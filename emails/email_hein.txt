I am finishing my MSc in Biomedical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology, and I would like to ask whether you have or expect a funded PhD position in your group.

Your division's recent work on sensor-based and machine learning assessment of older adults, such as the chair rise test, is the setting my thesis was built for. My thesis is an always-on voice assistant for older adults. I designed a 75k-parameter wake-word model, trained it with int8 quantization-aware training and run it in real time on a Raspberry Pi. Under five-fold cross-validation on held-out recordings and unseen speakers it was the most accurate model on every fold against DS-CNN, TC-ResNet8, BC-ResNet and DS-CNNs matched to its size and compute. In deployment the device microphone tripled false alarms while AUC barely moved, and retraining on hard negatives recorded on the device cut false activations from 60 to 3 in a 388-hour test.

This work has produced a system paper accepted at ICBME 2026 (IEEE), a compact-model paper submitted to ICEE 2027 and a journal paper submitted to IEEE Sensors Journal. I also work as an embedded and ML systems engineer. My CV is attached and my code is at github.com/MGE-GOAT.
