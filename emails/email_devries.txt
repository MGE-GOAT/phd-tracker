Dear Prof. de Vries,

I am finishing my MSc in Biomedical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology, and I would like to ask whether you expect a PhD position in BIASlab in the coming year.

Your work on Bayesian hearing agents that personalise audio processing in the field, such as AIDA, speaks to a problem I met in my own thesis. I built an always-on voice assistant for older adults with a 75k-parameter wake-word model that runs in int8 on a Raspberry Pi. It was the most accurate model on every fold of a five-fold cross-validation against DS-CNN, TC-ResNet8, BC-ResNet and size-matched and compute-matched baselines. But once it ran on the real device, the microphone tripled false alarms while AUC barely moved, and only retraining on sounds recorded on that device fixed it, from 60 false activations to 3 in a 388-hour test. Adapting an audio system to its user and device after deployment, instead of retraining offline, is what I would like to work on.

The work has produced a system paper accepted at ICBME 2026 (IEEE), a compact-model paper submitted to ICEE 2027 and a journal paper submitted to IEEE Sensors Journal. I also work as an embedded and ML systems engineer.

If a funded position might open, I would be glad to apply. My CV is attached and my code is at github.com/MGE-GOAT.
