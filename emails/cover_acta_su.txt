Dear Dr. Su,

I am applying for the PhD position on AI for early detection of cardiometabolic diseases using oral health data, in the MYDIAG.AI project at ACTA. I am finishing my MSc in Biomedical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology. I want to build health AI that is validated properly and then used in care, and your project asks for exactly that.

My thesis was a complete health system for older adults, an always-on voice assistant with a caregiver app. I designed its wake-word model and ran it in real time on a Raspberry Pi. I validated it carefully against several baselines and then tested it in real use. There the microphone changed how the system behaved in a way the offline tests had not shown, and I fixed it by retraining on data recorded on the device. This taught me that validation on retrospective data is only the first step. Prospective testing in the real setting is what shows whether a model helps. Your recent systematic review of randomised trials of AI in oral healthcare points at the same gap.

I also built the rest of the system. A server runs speech recognition and a language model for voice commands, and a Flutter app gives caregivers alerts, reminders and calls. I am used to combining several data sources and model types in one product, and to explaining technical results to people without an AI background.

This work produced three papers. One is accepted at ICBME 2026, one is submitted to the IEEE Sensors Journal and one to ICEE 2027. I work in Python and C++, and I have trained and deployed models with TensorFlow and TensorFlow Lite. I have not worked with dental data yet, and I would be glad to learn it inside a multidisciplinary team.

My CV, transcripts and the names of two academic references are attached. Thank you for considering my application.
