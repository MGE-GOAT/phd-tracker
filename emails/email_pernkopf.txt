Dear Prof. Pernkopf,

I am finishing my MSc in Biomedical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology, and I am writing to ask whether you expect a PhD position in your group in the coming year.

My thesis is an always-on wake-word system for older adults. I designed a 75k-parameter model, trained it with int8 quantization-aware training and run it in real time on a Raspberry Pi. Under five-fold cross-validation on held-out recordings and unseen speakers it was the most accurate model on every fold against DS-CNN, TC-ResNet8, BC-ResNet and DS-CNNs matched to its size and compute. In deployment the device microphone tripled false alarms while AUC barely moved, and retraining on device-recorded hard negatives cut false activations from 60 to 3 in a 388-hour test. Resource-efficient speech models and their robustness on real devices are what I would like to work on next, which is why your recent work on lightweight streaming speech encoders and on efficient Bayesian neural networks interests me.

The work has led to a system paper accepted at ICBME 2026 (IEEE), a compact-model paper submitted to ICEE 2027 and a journal paper submitted to IEEE Sensors Journal. I also work as an embedded and ML systems engineer.

If a funded position might open, I would be glad to apply. My CV is attached and my code is at github.com/MGE-GOAT. My German is intermediate.
