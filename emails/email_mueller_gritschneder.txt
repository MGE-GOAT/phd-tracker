I am finishing my MSc in Biomedical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology, and I would like to ask whether you have a funded PhD position in Embedded Computing Systems, for example within the DOMINO project on a green and safe edge AI lifecycle.

Your recent work on generating kernels so ML compilers can target edge accelerators, and on compressing models for efficient inference, matches what I have been building. My thesis is an always-on voice assistant for older adults. I designed a 75k-parameter wake-word model, trained it with int8 quantization-aware training and run it in real time on a Raspberry Pi with C++ and TFLite. Under five-fold cross-validation it beat DS-CNN, TC-ResNet8, BC-ResNet and DS-CNNs matched to its size and compute on every fold. I also measured what happens after deployment, where the device microphone tripled false alarms while AUC barely moved, and retraining on device-recorded hard negatives cut false activations from 60 to 3 in a 388-hour test. Keeping an edge model correct and efficient through its life on the device is the part I want to study.

The work has led to a system paper accepted at ICBME 2026 (IEEE), a compact-model paper submitted to ICEE 2027 and a journal paper submitted to IEEE Sensors Journal. I work as an embedded and ML systems engineer as well.
