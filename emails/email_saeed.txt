I am finishing my MSc in Biomedical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology, and I would like to ask whether there is a PhD position in your group, for example within your AiNed project on clinical audio understanding.

Your work on audio for personal health and on decentralized, on-device learning is very close to my thesis. I built a voice assistant for older adults that listens on the device and sends nothing until a wake word fires. I designed a 75k-parameter wake-word model, trained it with int8 quantization-aware training and run it in real time on a Raspberry Pi. Under five-fold cross-validation on held-out recordings and unseen speakers it was the most accurate model on every fold against DS-CNN, TC-ResNet8, BC-ResNet and DS-CNNs matched to its size and compute. In deployment the device microphone tripled false alarms while AUC barely moved, and retraining on hard negatives recorded on the device cut false activations from 60 to 3 in a 388-hour test.

This work has produced a system paper accepted at ICBME 2026 (IEEE), a compact-model paper submitted to ICEE 2027 and a journal paper submitted to IEEE Sensors Journal. I also work as an embedded and ML systems engineer and on production LLM agents, so I am comfortable with both the model and the device side of health audio.
