Subject: Motivation letter, PhD Fellow in Lifelong Machine Learning for Human Activity Recognition

Dear selection committee,

I am applying for the PhD fellowship in lifelong machine learning for human activity recognition. I completed the requirements for my MSc in Biomedical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology on 23 September 2026.

Why this position
My thesis was a home system for older adults, an always-on voice assistant that runs in real time on a Raspberry Pi and was tested in real rooms. Your group works in the same kind of setting. Smart Objects for Smart Care collects wearable and physiological data in real households, and you ask how a system can keep learning without sending personal data to a central server. That question came straight out of my own work.

How my qualifications match
The most important thing I learned is that a model that scores well offline can fail on real hardware. I recorded difficult cases on the device and retrained, and I learned how to measure this kind of shift and how to design a validation that matches real use. I can ship a model in C++ and Python, and I build tool-using LLM agents as an applied AI scientist. My thesis led to three papers. One is accepted at ICBME 2026, one is submitted to the IEEE Sensors Journal and one to ICEE 2027.

My research ideas
My thesis showed me that a model that works in the lab can fail in a real home. I would like to spend the PhD on three connected questions. How can a device notice that its sensor, its user or its home has changed, without anyone labelling new data? How can it then collect its own difficult cases and adapt without forgetting what it already knows? And if many homes learn together by sharing only model updates, how much accuracy do we give up for the privacy we gain? I do not have the answers yet, but these are the questions I would like to work on.

I have not yet worked on continual or federated learning in depth, and I would like to build that knowledge in your group. My CV, transcripts and master's thesis are in the application. Grades are on the Iranian 0 to 20 scale and I can send a conversion note if you need one.
