Subject: Motivation letter, PhD Fellow in Signal Processing for Medical Simulation

Dear selection committee,

I am applying for the PhD fellowship in signal processing for medical simulation in the Cardiac Arrest Physiology Simulation project. I completed the requirements for my MSc in Biomedical Engineering, with a bioelectric specialization, at Sharif University of Technology on 23 September 2026.

Why this position
I want to do signal processing and statistics on a real medical problem. Your project builds a patient model from real resuscitation recordings, so that training simulators reflect how hard a cardiac arrest really is. That is a clear problem with a direct use, and it will run inside Laerdal's simulators, so the model has to work in real time. Building small models that run reliably on real devices is what my thesis was about.

How my qualifications match
My thesis was an always-on voice assistant for older adults. I designed a compact model that runs in real time on a Raspberry Pi, with a streaming signal processing front end written in C++. Offline scores looked good, but real use showed a gap, and I studied it and retrained on data from the device. I know how to work with noisy recordings, how to separate the effect of the signal from the effect of the model, and how to validate honestly. My thesis led to three papers. One is accepted at ICBME 2026, one is submitted to the IEEE Sensors Journal and one to ICEE 2027.

My research ideas
First, I would estimate how a rhythm changes from one state to the next, for example from asystole to ventricular fibrillation to a shock, using a model in which the chance of each change depends on measured treatment quality such as compression depth and pauses. Second, I would check this model against held-out recordings and test whether its probabilities are well calibrated. Third, I would keep the model light enough to run in real time inside the simulator and test it there.

I have not yet worked with cardiac arrest recordings or ECG rhythm analysis, and I would like to learn them in a group that has done this for years. My CV, transcripts and master's thesis are in the application. Grades are on the Iranian 0 to 20 scale and I can send a conversion note if you need one.
