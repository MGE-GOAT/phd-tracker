I am finishing my MSc in Biomedical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology and I am writing to ask whether you expect an open PhD position in your group, for example through Hearing4all or a project grant.

Your work on multilingual query-by-example keyword spotting and on speech processing for hearing research is close to my thesis. I built an always-on Persian wake-word system for older adults with a 75k-parameter model that runs in int8 on a Raspberry Pi. Under five-fold cross-validation on held-out recordings and unseen speakers it was the most accurate model on every fold against DS-CNN, TC-ResNet8, BC-ResNet and DS-CNNs matched to its size and compute. In deployment I found that the device microphone tripled false alarms while AUC barely moved, and retraining on device-recorded hard negatives cut false activations from 60 to 3 in a 388-hour test.

The work has led to a system paper accepted at ICBME 2026 (IEEE), a compact-model paper submitted to ICEE 2027 and a journal paper submitted to IEEE Sensors Journal. I also work as an embedded and ML systems engineer.
