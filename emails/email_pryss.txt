I am finishing my MSc in Biomedical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology, and I would like to ask whether you expect a funded PhD position in Medical Data Science.

Your recent work on just-in-time adaptive interventions for patients at home is close to the setting of my thesis, an assistive device that has to work in real homes. My thesis is an always-on voice assistant for older adults. I designed a 75k-parameter wake-word model, trained it with int8 quantization-aware training and run it in real time on a Raspberry Pi. Under five-fold cross-validation on held-out recordings and unseen speakers it was the most accurate model on every fold against DS-CNN, TC-ResNet8, BC-ResNet and DS-CNNs matched to its size and compute. In deployment the device microphone tripled false alarms while AUC barely moved, and retraining on hard negatives recorded on the device cut false activations from 60 to 3 in a 388-hour test. Your work on vocal biomarkers is where I would like to take this next.
